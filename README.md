## 👋 About The Project

**BISP Clicker** is a fun, educational idle clicker game themed around the **British International School Phuket (BISP)**. 

This project was created by **Dima Kumankov**, a Year 3 student in class 3PW, as a way to learn about coding and game design. With a little help from AI and his Dad, Dima built a game where you can grow your own BISP campus!

Start by clicking to study, then hire students and teachers, build real BISP facilities, and eventually graduate to earn prestige bonuses!

## ✨ Features

### 🎓 Progression System
- **Students**: From Year 1 to University Professors! Hire students to automatically generate IQ.
- **Teachers**: Hire staff like Teaching Assistants and Principals to boost your students' learning.
- **Facilities**: Build iconic BISP locations like the **MTB Library**, **Olympic Pool**, and **Aerial Arts Academy**.

### 🚀 Gameplay Mechanics
- **Idle Growth**: Your school keeps growing even when you're not clicking!
- **Upgrades**: Buy "Better Pencils", "Ergonomic Desks", and "Study Music" to click faster.
- **Prestige**: Reach 1 Million IQ to **GRADUATE**! Reset your progress to earn Certificates and permanent multipliers.
- **Achievements**: Unlock trophies for reaching milestones.

### 🌟 Fun Extras
- **Combo multiplier**: Tap fast to build a streak. At 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 taps you get x1.25 / x1.5 / x2 / x3 IQ per click.
- **Golden School Badge**: A 🏅 appears at random. Catch it for **x3.5 IQ/s for 30 seconds**! (The first one never shows up in the first 3 minutes.)
- **Campus stages**: Your school grows from a Study Corner to a Genius City as you earn IQ.
- **Milestones**: Confetti, sounds and toasts when you reach big IQ or click numbers.
- **While you were away**: Come back to a "Welcome back!" screen showing the IQ earned offline (up to 24 hours).

### 🎨 Design
- **Beautiful UI**: Modern, glassmorphism design with smooth animations, click particles, flying numbers and purchase toasts.
- **Sound**: All sounds are synthesized with the Web Audio API (no audio files). Toggle with 🔊 in the top bar. On phones, sound starts after your first tap.
- **Sticky HUD**: IQ, IQ/s and click bonus are always visible at the top.
- **Responsive**: Works on computers, tablets and phones. On phones the shop is a bottom sheet with tabs, the STUDY button sits in the thumb zone, touch targets are at least 44px, and iPhone safe areas and landscape are supported.
- **BISP Themed**: Uses the school's colors and real locations.

### ⚙️ Settings
- Sound on/off and game stats.
- **Reset all progress**: a red button at the very bottom of Settings. It needs two separate confirmations.

### 💾 Saves
Progress is stored in your browser (`localStorage`). The save format is backwards compatible: older saves load fine and new fields are filled in automatically.



## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: For the game structure.
- **CSS3**: For the beautiful styling and animations.
- **JavaScript (ES6)**: For the game logic and math.
- **No Frameworks**: Pure, vanilla code!
